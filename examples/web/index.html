FLIP in the browser

LDR-FLIP estimates how visible the differences between two sRGB images are to a viewer flipping between them. This page runs the flip-rs crate compiled to WebAssembly; images never leave your machine. Drop or choose two images of equal size. Alpha is ignored.

The sample pair is procedural: the test image has fewer samples per pixel, noise, a blurred sign and a shifted sky color. The default of 67 pixels per degree corresponds to a 0.7 m wide 4K monitor viewed from 0.7 m. The mean is computed here in double precision and may differ in the last digits from the crate's f32 pooling.
